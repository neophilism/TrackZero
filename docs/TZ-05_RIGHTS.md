# TZ-05 publication guard

The branch now includes a local SQLite-backed rights review ledger and a framework-neutral HTTP boundary. A server-verified artist or label may submit an item-and-region claim with a digest of privately stored evidence. A separate server-verified rights reviewer may approve or deny it for a bounded time; self-review is denied. Withdrawal revokes approval, and every transition records an audit event without raw rights documents. Client-supplied identity or reviewer roles cannot authorize a transition. `node --test tests/rights-registry.test.mjs` covers approval, expiry, withdrawal, role/region isolation, injected write interruption and HTTP denial locally.

This is a software review workflow, not a legal opinion, licensed broadcaster, production rights registry or operational publishing path. A real identity provider, private evidence store, authenticated reviewer workflow, jurisdiction and ownership checks, takedown/dispute process, DRE-05 enforcement, and product CI still require integration and evidence. The original TZ-05 plan depends on TZ-04 and DRE-05; imported dependency arrays alone do not prove those prerequisites. Missing approvals fail closed. Never infer a license from artist metadata or past uploads.
